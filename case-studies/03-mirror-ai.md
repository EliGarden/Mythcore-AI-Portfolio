# Mirror AI | A legible hypothesis remains a hypothesis

**Selected version:** 1.3.0 Relational Interpretation. **Capability demonstrated:** epistemic separation and explainable representations. This is a bounded reference extension, not an empirically validated model of cognition.

The problem is that a vivid explanation can look authoritative even when it is only one interpretation of limited observations. The package distinguishes observations, hypotheses, alternatives and unknowns; binds them to source/provenance records; and supports a faithful read-only view. Candidate annotations have no apply operation. [M1]

**Included synthetic example:** `ai_behavior_bundle.json` labels an unexpected sword metaphor in a synthetic teaching trace as an observation. 'Prompt carryover' is a candidate explanation, not a diagnosis or discovered mechanism. The example marks empirical claims false and calibrated probability false. The observation does not establish which explanation generalizes. The portfolio is reading an existing authored fixture, not reporting model telemetry or a new model experiment. [M2]

The reference service validates authored graph data. A generative hypothesis provider, trusted live profile adapters and empirical Gaia calibration are not implemented. A visually coherent graph therefore cannot be offered as evidence that the explanation is true. The inherited fifteen-faculty runtime remains distinct from this extension. [M1]

The historical record reports 108 passing successor tests, following reproduction of an inherited expired-fixture failure and a test-only epoch correction. It does not demonstrate interpretation accuracy or production trust. The contradiction ledger retains an open external Gaia-fixture compatibility issue and unresolved adapter/empirical questions. No external canonical pointer was changed by the release. [M3, M4]

**Trade-off and relevance:** an explicit graph makes alternatives and evidence inspectable, but creates annotation burden and a risk of reifying symbols. An AI team can assess whether the interface encourages appropriate uncertainty without assuming that symbolic richness adds scientific validity.
